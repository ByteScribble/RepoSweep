# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, harassment-free project for everyone.

Be respectful, assume good intent while evaluating technical claims by evidence, accept constructive feedback, and focus disagreement on ideas and implementation. Harassment, personal attacks, discrimination, doxxing, and sustained disruptive behavior are not acceptable.

Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and participation that violate these expectations. Serious incidents should be reported privately through the repository's maintainer or security contact.

This concise policy will be replaced by a formally adopted community standard before the first public release.
